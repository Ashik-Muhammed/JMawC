## 8. Link Hygiene, Safety & Anchor Text Rules

- **Brand Anchor Distribution**: Keep 65–70% of anchor texts branded (`Jayamahesh Ayurveda`, `Jayamahesh Clinic`, `jayamahesh.web.app`).
- **Partial / Descriptive Anchors**: 15–20% (`Ayurveda clinic in Parassala`, `Dr. Jayalekshmi Ayurvedic physician`, `Kerala Pizhichil treatment`).
- **Generic / URL Anchors**: 10–15% (`visit website`, `view therapies`, `source`).
- **Avoid PBNs & Link Farms**: Never purchase links from automated Fiverr/Upwork packages. All links must be from genuine health, regional, or lifestyle websites with real organic traffic.
- **Continuous Monitoring**: Track inbound links monthly using Google Search Console (Links report) and maintain a clean profile.
